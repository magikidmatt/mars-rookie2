The Shape Secret 🧊

⏱️ 5 Minutes — Logic

Why is Perseverance shaped like a box,
but Ingenuity the helicopter has a different shape? 🚁

🚁
Light & Skinny
Ingenuity is tiny — only 4 lbs!
It needs to be light to FLY
Helicopters love this! 🪶
📦
Big Box Shape
Perseverance is car-sized!
Holds cameras, drills, & arms
Rovers need this!
VS
⭐ The Big Idea: Different jobs need different shapes!

🚁 Ingenuity = Light & Small → Flies in thin air
🤖 Perseverance = Big Box → Carries tools on the ground

Both shapes are PERFECT for their mission!
